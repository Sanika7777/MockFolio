SEBI Learner Note SIMULATION METHODOLOGY

Simulated Order Matching & Price Impact

In real markets governed by SEBI, every order alters the depth of market (L2 book). In MockFolio, limit and market fills mimic this dynamic: trades adjust sandbox prices by an algorithmic liquidity slippage based on trade size. In addition, simulated brokerage of 0.1% reflects realistic net return expectations.
